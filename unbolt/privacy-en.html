Unbolt Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

This is the privacy policy for the Unbolt game for Android ("the game"), published by GSR Apps Studio (Gustavo Adolfo Salcedo Rodríguez, Colombia; "we", "us"). It explains what data the game processes, why, who it is shared with, how long we keep it, and how you can exercise your rights.

Data controller: GSR Apps Studio · Colombia · Privacy contact: gsrappstudio@gmail.com

1. Summary

  • Unbolt does not ask you to create an account, nor for your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, microphone, camera or precise location.
  • We identify you with an anonymous identifier generated when you open the game, to save your progress and coins in the cloud and to prevent cheating.
  • We measure how the game is used with analytics to improve it.
  • The game shows ads (with your consent where the law requires it) and offers in-app purchases through Google Play.
  • You can request deletion of your data at any time (section 8).

2. Age

Unbolt is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over and is not directed to children under that age. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13 (or the minimum age set by your country). If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

3. Data we process

Category Data Purpose
Identifiers Anonymous player ID (Unity Authentication) and installation ID (Unity Analytics). If you accept personalized ads, also your device advertising ID. Recognize you across sessions, save progress, measure usage, serve ads.
Game data Levels completed, coins, daily streak and reward, daily challenge results, settings (language, theme, sound). Cloud save, server-side economy, daily challenge.
Usage analytics Game events: level start, completion, failure or abandonment, errors, use of hints and undo, daily challenge, consent status, app version. Understand which levels are hard, balance the game, fix bugs.
Device data Model, operating system, language, app version, approximate country (derived from IP address), crash logs and diagnostics. Operation, compatibility, statistics and bug fixing.
Ads Data Google AdMob collects to serve and measure ads: advertising ID, IP address, device data, ad interactions. Show ads and fund the game.
Purchases Purchase identifier and status (Google Play token). We do not receive your card or payment method details. Deliver purchases, restore purchases, prevent fraud.

We do not collect: name, email, phone number, address, contacts, messages, photos, videos, audio, precise location, health, financial or biometric data.

4. Who we share data with

Only with service providers, under their own privacy terms:

  • Unity Technologies (Unity Gaming Services): anonymous authentication, cloud save, server-side economy, remote configuration and analytics. Unity Privacy Policy.
  • Google (AdMob, Google Play, Google Play Billing, UMP): ads, consent and purchases. Google Privacy Policy.

We do not sell your data and do not use it for profiling beyond the purposes described.

5. Consent and ads

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland or another region that requires it, when you open the game you will see a consent form (Google UMP) where you choose whether to accept personalized ads and analytics. You can change your choice at any time in the game's Settings → Privacy options. If you do not accept personalized ads you will see non-personalized ads. You can also reset or limit your advertising ID in Android settings (Google → Ads).

6. Legal basis (EEA/UK users)

Performance of the service you request (saving progress, economy, purchases), your consent (personalized ads, analytics where required, which you can withdraw at any time), and our legitimate interest in security, fraud prevention and improving the game.

7. Retention

  • Game data (progress, coins): while you use the game and until you request deletion.
  • Analytics and diagnostics: kept for the period applied by the provider (Unity), then deleted or anonymized.
  • Purchase data: for as long as legal and tax obligations require.
  • After a verified deletion request, we delete your game data within 30 days at most and ask the provider to delete the analytics data.

8. Data deletion and your rights

You can request the following at any time, without needing an account:

  1. Delete your data: email gsrappstudio@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Unbolt data" and include the player ID shown in the game's Settings (so we can locate your information). You can also use this policy's data deletion web page: https://gsrappstudio.github.io/unbolt/delete-data.html.
  2. Access, correct or receive a copy of your data, object to or restrict its processing.
  3. Withdraw your consent in Settings → Privacy options.

We reply within 30 days. If your data is deleted, you will lose your progress and coins and they cannot be recovered. We may keep the minimum data required for legal obligations, security or fraud prevention, and we will tell you. Uninstalling the game does not delete data saved in the cloud.

Depending on where you live, you also have the rights granted by the GDPR (EU/UK), LGPD (Brazil), CCPA/CPRA (California) and your country's data protection laws, including the right to lodge a complaint with your supervisory authority. In Colombia you have the rights of Law 1581 of 2012 and may complain to the Superintendencia de Industria y Comercio (SIC). We do not sell or share your personal data for cross-context behavioral advertising, except as described in section 5.

9. Security

Data is transmitted encrypted (HTTPS). The economy and purchases are validated on the server to prevent tampering. No system is 100% secure, but we apply reasonable measures and restrict access to data.

10. International transfers

Our providers (Unity, Google) may process data in countries other than yours, including the United States, with the safeguards they establish (for example, standard contractual clauses).

11. Changes

If we change this policy, we will publish the new version at this address with its update date and, for important changes, notify you in the game.
