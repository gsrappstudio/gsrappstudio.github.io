Privacy Policy — Puzzle-gsr

Last updated: October 29, 2026 App: Puzzle-gsr (Android, com.gsr.puzzlegsr) Controller: Gustavo Adolfo Salcedo Rodríguez, publishing as "GSR Apps Studio", based in Colombia. Contact: gsrappstudio@gmail.com

1. In short

Puzzle-gsr is a jigsaw puzzle game. You do not need an account. Your progress, saved games and photos stay on your phone. The app shows ads and offers in-app purchases; the Google services that make this possible may collect some data, as explained below. We do not sell your data.

2. Data stored on your phone

  • Settings: language, color theme, orientation and game options.
  • Progress: games in progress and completed, times, moves, hints used, records and stars. Stored in a local file and never sent to our servers (we do not run user servers).
  • Your photos: if you choose "My photos", the app reads only the image you select to turn it into a puzzle. It is processed on your phone, not uploaded and not shared. The app keeps a reduced copy in its private storage so you can replay it; it is deleted when you delete the game or uninstall the app.
  • Purchases: the app remembers locally what you have unlocked (for example, "Remove ads").

You can erase your history in Settings → Clear history, or everything by uninstalling the app.

3. Data collected by third-party services

Service What it may collect Why
Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) Android advertising ID, IP address, device model and OS version, language, ad interactions, approximate location, diagnostics Show and measure ads, prevent fraud and, if you agree, personalize them
Google Play Billing Order ID, product purchased, purchase status. We never receive your card details Process and restore purchases
Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) Your consent choice Ask for and remember your ad consent

These services handle data under their own policies: Google Privacy Policy and how Google uses data from apps that use its services.

We do not use our own analytics or any third-party tracking tools other than those listed.

4. Ads and your consent

  • The app shows rewarded ads (for example, to get an extra hint or add a photo) and full-screen ads after some puzzles. Buying "Remove ads" stops them.
  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, we ask for your consent before showing personalized ads. You can change your choice any time in Settings → Privacy options.
  • If you decline personalization, you will see non-personalized ads.
  • On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in System settings → Google → Ads.

5. In-app purchases

Handled by Google Play. To restore purchases on another phone, open the app with the same Google account and use the restore option. Refunds follow Google Play policies.

6. How we use data

To make the game work, show ads and measure their performance, process purchases, prevent fraud and meet legal obligations. Where the law requires it (for example personalized ads in the EU), the legal basis is your consent, which you can withdraw.

7. Children

Puzzle-gsr is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their personal data. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it as far as we are able.

8. Who we share data with

Only the Google services listed above, and authorities where legally required. Those providers may process data in other countries.

9. Retention

Local data stays until you delete it or uninstall the app. Data handled by Google services follows their retention policies.

10. Your rights

You can ask us for access, correction or deletion of your data, and withdraw your consent. Because the app does not keep your data on our servers, most actions are done by you on the phone itself (clear history, uninstall, reset advertising ID). For any request or complaint, write to gsrappstudio@gmail.com. Detailed steps are on the Delete your data page. In Colombia you may also contact the Superintendencia de Industria y Comercio (Law 1581 of 2012).

11. Security

Communications with Google services are encrypted. The app does not store passwords or payment data.

12. Changes

Any change will be posted here with a new date. For important changes we will notify you in the app.
